Welcome to Ladder Records

Ladder Records is a creative collective, label, and recording hub based around Muscle Memory Musik Studio. We work with artists, producers, and collectives to bring projects from concept to release.

About

Founded with the ethos of “We can get you to the next rung of the ladder,” Ladder Records supports the full creative journey — from songwriting, recording, development, and live performance.
