Muscle Memory Musik Studio

Founded in 2005, Muscle Memory Musik Studio is a creative hub for artists, producers, and youth programs. The studio has hosted collaborations, recordings, and artist development projects that have led to Ladder Records and its collective of artists.

Studio

History

The studio was built from the ground up, with producers and engineers Young Max and Del aka Sonic forming the initial core alongside Louis aka Reason.

Equipment Overview

  • Electric, acoustic, rare, handmade, vintage, and modern guitars
  • Fender and Rowland keys
  • Marshall amps
  • State-of-the-art audio interface and recording equipment
