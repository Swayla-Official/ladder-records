Muscle Memory Musik Studio

Muscle Memory Musik Studio is the creative headquarters of Ladder Records. Over time it has developed into a collaborative hub for recording, filming, podcasting, songwriting and artist development.

Studio Timeline

2010
Early collaborative recordings including Panning for Gold.
2013
Recording work with Cockney Rejects.
2017
Singer-songwriter Serena Dench records at the studio.
2021
Projects connected to Mostwanted2.
2022
Sessions with Tia Pearman and Courtney Courtney.
2024
Sing Out Loud Sundays uses the studio as HQ.
2025–2026
Muscle Memory becomes the official home of GZONE — Rap Battle. Curated by GingaJay, the platform showcases lyricists clashing in a new entertainment format.
