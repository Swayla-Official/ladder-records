Founded in 2005, built from the ground up. The studio has fostered collaborations with a wide range of artists.

Studio History

2005

Studio founded. Creative freedom prioritized for artists.

Early Foundations

Producers and engineers Young Max and Del aka Sonic were pivotal in early collaborations, forming the initial trio alongside Louis aka Reason.

2012

Dishy Tangent formed by Reason and Matt Shilk, genre-busting project with live instrumentation.

2014

Sing Out Loud Sundays founded by RM Moses. Studio becomes HQ for recordings and sessions.
