Muscle Memory Musik Studio

Muscle Memory Musik Studio is the creative headquarters of Ladder Records, supporting recording, songwriting, podcasting and artist development.

Studio Timeline

2010
Early recording projects including Panning for Gold.
2013
Recording work with Cockney Rejects.
2017
Singer songwriter Serena Dench records at the studio.
2021
Projects linked to Mostwanted2.
2022
Sessions with Tia Pearman and Courtney Courtney.
2024
Sing Out Loud Sundays founded by RM Moses uses the studio as filming HQ.
2025-2026
Muscle Memory becomes the official home of GZONE Rap Battle. Curated by GingaJay, the platform showcases UK lyricists clashing in a new independent entertainment format.

Young Max and Del aka Sonic were pivotal in early collaborations and formed the initial trio of producers and engineers of Muscle Memory Studio alongside Louis aka Reason.

  • Fender electric guitars

  • Marshall amplifiers
